About

Mark59 originated from ideas conceived around 2014 by a small team of Performance Testers working at an Australian insurance company. We wanted a way to use existing UI functional test scripts available to us, and to reliably track performance trends over time. We believe Mark59 still offers capabilities not easily available in other solutions. It's also simple to adopt as it can sit within an existing JMeter framework, and can be adopted as needed.

The name 'Mark59' comes from a play on words from a biblical reference (Mark 5:9), where Jesus asks a demon-possessed man his name, and the reply "My name is Legion, for we are many" implies a multitude instead of the expected singular. Kind of analogous to the way Mark59 handles Playwright and Selenium tests.
