About

Mark59 originated from ideas conceived around 2014 from a small team of us working as Performance Testers at IAG, a mid-sized insurance company in Australia. We wanted a way to use existing functional scripts available to us, and a way to reliably track performance trends over time. We believe it still offers capabilities not easliy available in other solutions. It's also simple to adopt as it can sit within an existing JMeter framework, and can be adopted as needed.

The name 'Mark59' comes from a play on words from a biblical reference (Mark 5:9), where Jesus asks a demon-possessed man his name, and the reply "My name is Legion, for we are many" implies a multitude instead of the expected singular. Kind of analogous to the way Mark59 handles Playwright and Selenium tests.
